# Screen-recording walkthrough

Target length: 2–3 minutes. Open `app/index.html` before recording. Keep the “Local demo mode” banner visible when showing actions so the recording cannot be mistaken for live email delivery.

1. Show **Overview** and call out the lead count, strong-fit count, follow-up queue, and pipeline statuses.
2. Open **Prospects**. Point out that the synthetic records are sorted by transparent fit score. Open Maya Green and show the stored public-profile fields and initial email draft.
3. Tick the review checkbox and show that **Log approved initial demo** becomes available. Do not describe it as an email send; the app will only add a local activity entry.
4. Open Rohan Reuse, show the due follow-up and its draft, then demonstrate the approval checkbox and demo logging.
5. Open **Replies**, select **Add demo reply**, and submit an interested response. Show its classification and the updated CRM status.
6. Open the reply draft for a prospect, then show campaign setup and the export controls.

Suggested narration: “This prototype demonstrates the workflow with synthetic records. It ranks prospects using visible rules and template drafts, then requires review before a demo action is logged. No Instagram profile is scraped and no email is sent. A live version would connect authorized accounts while preserving the same approval and stop rules.”
