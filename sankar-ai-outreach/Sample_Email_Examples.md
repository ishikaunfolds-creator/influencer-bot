## Stop rules

- Any incoming reply stops both pending follow-ups.
- A decline updates the status to `Not Interested` and stops outreach.
- An opt-out sets the suppression flag, updates the status to `Not Interested`, and stops outreach.
- Every generated reply remains a draft until a human reviews it. The local demo does not send it.
